- **Page-Level Composition**: The module is structured around three primary route components: `MyCircle` (personal relationship CRM), `Connections` (social graph management), and `Messages` (real-time chat).
- **State Management**: Utilizes a centralized Zustand store (`useMessagingStore`) for complex messaging state (conversations, active chat, tri-chat mediator status), while `MyCircle` relies on local component state for its multi-tab interface (People, Rituals, Events, Tensions, Graph, Review).
- **Real-Time Integration**: `Messages.tsx` implements a comprehensive Socket.IO client integration within a `useEffect` hook, handling events for typing indicators, message delivery/read receipts, reactions, and AI mediator streaming chunks.
- **AI Mediation Layer**: Features a unique "Tri-Chat" architecture where an AI mediator can be summoned into a dyadic conversation, with UI support for streaming responses, action cards (e.g., suggest ritual, log tension), and session management.
- **Sub-Component Delegation**: `MyCircle.tsx` delegates complex tab content to specialized sub-components (e.g., `RitualsTab`, `GraphTab`, `AnnualReviewTab`) to maintain readability and separation of concerns within the monolithic page file.
